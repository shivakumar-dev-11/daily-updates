Virat Kohli is one of the most famous and successful cricketers in the world. He was born on November 5, 1988, in Delhi, India. Kohli is known for his aggressive batting style, consistency, and strong leadership skills. He has played for the India national cricket team in all formats of cricket and has served as the team’s captain in the past. 🏏 Kohli made his international debut in 2008 and quickly became one of the best batsmen in modern cricket. He has scored many centuries across Test matches, One Day Internationals (ODIs), and T20 matches. Because of his fitness, dedication, and passion for the game, he is often considered one of the greatest batsmen of his generation. In the Indian Premier League (IPL), Kohli has been a key player for the Royal Challengers Bengaluru. He holds several IPL records, including being one of the highest run-scorers in the tournament’s history. His outstanding performances have also helped India in major tournaments such as the ICC Cricket World Cup. Apart from cricket, Kohli is admired for his discipline, fitness, and charity work. He inspires millions of young people to stay healthy, work hard, and follow their dreams.
